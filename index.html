Official Story Manual
Character Profiles
Elsa
The elegant Crown Princess born with magical control over ice and winter storms. She wrestles with isolation as she learns to control her legendary powers.
Anna
Elsa's fiercely loyal, highly optimistic younger sister. Energetic, free-spirited, and quick to action, she ventures into the frozen wilderness to save her kingdom.
Kristoff
A rugged ice harvester living deep in the mountains. Though rough around the edges, his bond with his reindeer companion Sven highlights his dependable heart.
Olaf
A magical, innocent snowman brought to life by Elsa's subconscious winter powers. He loves warm hugs and represents the childhood love between the two sisters.
Sven
Kristoff's loyal reindeer sidekick and best friend. He possesses a strong moral compass and acts as Kristoff's trusted conscience throughout their journey.
